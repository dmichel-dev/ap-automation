Privacy notice

Demo at demo.davidmichel.dev, run by David Michel, freelancer. Contact: david@davidmichel.dev

Send fictional invoices only. Please don't send real invoices or anyone's personal data.

What the demo receives

  • The files you upload or email.
  • For an email: the sender's address, the subject and the text, used to find your session and to spot bank-detail change notices.
  • Your network address and the sender's address, only to apply the demo's limits. They are stored as keyed hashes, never as written, and deleted after 2 days.
  • One cookie that keeps your session open in this browser. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

What happens to your files

  • Your own files are read by the OCR service of Mistral AI (France), under its API terms. The sample invoices are not sent to Mistral.
  • The results are shown only on your session page, which opens only in the browser that started it.
  • Nothing is posted to QuickBooks or any other system. Nothing is shared or sold.

How long it is kept

  • A session ends 60 minutes after it starts. Its files, results and session data are then deleted.
  • Emails are moved to the mailbox's trash, which empties itself after 30 days.
  • An emailed file also stays in the workflow tool's run history (n8n) for 14 days.
  • The server's nightly backup (03:20 UTC) can hold a session that was open at that moment. Backups are kept for 14 days.
  • The processing log keeps dates, statuses and counts, never a document's content.

Your rights

Write to david@davidmichel.dev to ask what is held about you or to have it deleted. You can also complain to a data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
