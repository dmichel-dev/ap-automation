Privacy notice

Demo at demo.davidmichel.dev, run by David Michel, freelancer. Contact: contact@davidmichel.dev

Send fictional invoices only. Please don't send real invoices or anyone's personal data.

What the demo receives

  • The files you upload or email.
  • For an email: the sender's address, the subject and the text, used to find your session and to spot bank-detail change notices.
  • Your network address and the sender's address, only to apply the demo's limits. They are stored as keyed hashes, never as written, and deleted after 2 days.
  • One cookie that keeps your session open in this browser. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Why, and on what legal basis

To run the demo you start, and to protect it from abuse (limits, OCR budget). The legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)): running and protecting the demo.

What happens to your files

  • Your own files are read by the OCR service of Mistral AI (Paris, France) on my behalf. The sample invoices are not sent to Mistral.
  • By default, Mistral AI keeps API inputs and outputs for 30 rolling days to monitor abuse, unless zero data retention is active. For that purpose, Mistral AI is an independent controller: see Mistral AI's privacy policy. Zero data retention is not active for this demo.
  • Emails reach the demo through Google Workspace (Gmail), and the demo runs on a server hosted by OVHcloud. Both act on my behalf.
  • The results are shown only on your session page, which opens only in the browser that started it.
  • Nothing is posted to QuickBooks or any other system. Nothing is shared or sold.

How long it is kept

  • A session ends 60 minutes after it starts. Within a few minutes, its files, results and session data are deleted.
  • Its emails are then deleted permanently from the mailbox (not moved to the trash). An email the demo can't accept (session over, limit reached) is deleted at once.
  • An emailed file is deleted from the workflow tool (n8n) within 90 minutes of its arrival.
  • The workflow tool's records of the session's runs are deleted at the same time as the session. A run that fails before it reaches your session can stay in that history for up to 14 days, without the file.
  • The server's nightly backup (03:20 UTC) can hold a session that was open at that moment. Backups are kept for 14 days. Our host, OVHcloud, also keeps a copy of the whole server for 24 hours, so a backup copy can exist for up to 15 days in total.
  • The processing log keeps dates, statuses and counts, never a document's content.

Your rights

Write to contact@davidmichel.dev to ask what is held about you, to have it deleted, or to object to its processing. You can also complain to a data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
