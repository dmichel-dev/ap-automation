Send an invoice. Watch it become a QuickBooks bill.

Each invoice is read by OCR, checked against fixed rules (totals, tax, dates, duplicates, bank details), then either prepared as a bill or sent to a person for review. You see every step.

Today: someone types each invoice in by hand and checks totals, tax and bank details by eye. It's slow, and a changed bank account is easy to miss.

Automated: invoices are picked up where they arrive and checked by fixed rules. Clean ones go straight through; the rest go to a person, with the reason.
