Send an invoice. Watch it get posted to QuickBooks.

Each invoice is read by OCR, checked against fixed rules (totals, tax, dates, duplicates, bank details), then either prepared as a QuickBooks bill or sent to a person for review. You see every step. In this demo, nothing is sent to QuickBooks: you see the bill it would create.

Today: in many companies, invoices arrive through several channels (email, shared drives…), then each one is typed in by hand and checked by eye. It's slow, and a changed bank account can slip through.

Automated: invoices are picked up wherever they arrive, then read and checked automatically. Clean ones go straight to QuickBooks, a changed bank account is flagged, and the rest go to a person with the reason.
